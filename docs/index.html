    Giving shortlist

    Curated destinations, one per ecosystem. The first two act on the balance above — removal settles it, prevention complements it without settling — and the rest fund hands-on restoration, not offset accounting.

    • Air — removal Remove Carbon Today (biochar CORCs) Ex-post Puro-audited biochar, ~$160/t; stock rotates, sold out at times.
    • Air — prevention Tradewater Refrigerant and methane destruction, ~$15/t; does not settle the removal balance.
    • Rivers American Rivers Dam removal — about 4,900 river miles reconnected in 2025 alone.
    • Oceans Coral Restoration Foundation Coral propagation and outplanting in the Florida Keys.
    • Coasts & estuaries Billion Oyster Project Self-sustaining oyster reefs in NY Harbor — 150M+ oysters, 17 acres built.
    • Land & watershed Naturaland Trust Greenville land purchase protecting the Saluda and Reedy watersheds.
    • Wetlands & swamp Congaree Land Trust Easements around Congaree's blackwater swamp (COWASEE basin).
