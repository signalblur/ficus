One act, recorded as three streams. Each carries the same three readings: the lifetime total, what that total is comparable to, and how the latest month compares with the one before it. That last figure is identical on all three cards, and that is not a fault — carbon and water are derived from energy by fixed published factors, so the three always move together.

By month

The trace

Three lanes on one time axis, so a month reads across all three streams at once.

The three lanes are congruent by construction. Energy is the physical quantity; carbon and water are derived from it by the fixed published factors in the derivation below, so the shapes have to match. Drawing them together shows that rather than hiding it — what changes between lanes is the scale and the unit, never the shape.

    Receipts & books

    Every offset purchase and every donation, with the receipt attached.

    Everything a tax year needs, in one place: who paid, what was bought, what it cost, and the proof. Each purchase carries the SHA-256 of its receipt as recorded at filing time, the registry retirement ID where one was issued, and the receipt itself as a download built from the stored bytes — no server, no link to rot.     How these are made

    Derived, not measured

    Every figure on this page is derived from stored token counts by a published factor. Nothing is measured. Anthropic publishes no per-query energy data, so the honest description of all of it is order-of-magnitude, roughly ±50%. Trends and relative comparisons are meaningful; absolute values are indicative.

    Fuller derivations, the uncertainty bands per model family, and the cross-check gate against an independent estimator are in METHODOLOGY.md.
